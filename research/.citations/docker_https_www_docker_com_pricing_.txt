## Common questions

### Which plan do I need to run coding agents?

Docker Sandboxes is free for individual developers and gives every agent its own isolated microVM. When a team needs shared policy, visibility and audit logs across all of its agents, that is AI Governance.

### Is Docker Sandboxes really free?

Yes. Individual developers can run agents in sandboxes on their own machine at no cost. Centralized controls, MCP Gateway and org-wide policies are part of AI Governance, which is priced per organization.

### How are Hardened Images priced?

Community is free for everyone. Select and Enterprise are quoted on the repositories you customize and the support you need, with no cap on users. Talk to sales for a quote that matches your catalog.

### What counts as a user on the Teams plan?

Anyone you add to your Docker organization. Teams starts at two users and is billed per user per month, with the option to add users in bulk up to 100.

### Can I mix plans across my organization?

Yes. Hardened Images, AI Governance and Docker Desktop plans are separate subscriptions, so a team can run Select images on a Teams plan, or add AI Governance to an existing Custom agreement.

### What happens when I reach the free pull-rate limit?

Free accounts get 100 Docker Hub pulls an hour. Pulls pause until the hour resets. Teams and Custom plans remove the limit entirely.

### Do you offer annual billing?

Teams can be billed monthly or annually, and annual billing is discounted. Custom and Enterprise agreements are annual by default.

### How do I add Premium Support or a TAM?

Premium Support and a technical advisory manager are add-ons to Docker Business, DHI Enterprise and AI Governance. Your account team can attach them to an existing agreement at any time.
