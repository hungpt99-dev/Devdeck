### For students and teachers free

Students and academic staff members are eligible to use all JetBrains tools free, upon verification of their university/college domain email or ISIC card.

[Learn more](https://www.jetbrains.com/academy/student-pack/)

### For classroom assistance free

Universities, colleges, schools, and non-commercial educational organizations are eligible for free licensing to install all JetBrains tools in classrooms and computer labs for educational purposes.

[Learn more](https://www.jetbrains.com/academy/teacher-pack/)

### For Open Source projects free

Core maintainers of open-source projects are welcome to explore collaboration opportunities and receive free access to JetBrains tools.

[Learn more](https://www.jetbrains.com/community/opensource/#support)

### For universities and educational organizations 50% off

Universities and accredited educational institutions can take advantage of our 50% discount on commercial subscriptions for development of internal projects and applications. Please contact sales to request a quote.

[Learn more](https://www.jetbrains.com/support/sales/)

### For startups 50% off

Private software development companies which have been in business for less than 5 years may qualify for a 50% discount for up to 10 commercial licenses on any JetBrains products.

[Learn more](https://www.jetbrains.com/startups/#startup-program)

### For former student license holders 40% off

Student license holders can get a 40% Graduation discount, which can be used towards the purchase of a new personal subscription for any single JetBrains product or for the All Products pack. The same 40% Graduation discount is offered to students of coding bootcamps and training courses who have used free coupon codes during their training.

[Learn more](https://www.jetbrains.com/academy/student-pack/)

### For Developer Recognition Program free

Active Microsoft MVPs, ASPInsiders, Java Champions, Docker Captains, Gradle Fellows, Google Developer Experts, Embarcadero MVPs, and Nx Champions are eligible to receive a free All Products Pack subscription upon verification of their profile.

[Learn more](https://www.jetbrains.com/community/dev-recognition/)

### For non-profit organizations 50% off

Registered non-profit organizations can apply for a special discount ranging from 25% to 50% off commercial pricing, depending on their business model. Please contact sales to request a quote.

[Learn more](https://www.jetbrains.com/support/sales/)

### For developer communities free

Organizers of active developer communities can get JetBrains support, including IDE subscriptions, swag, content, speakers, sponsorship, and visibility.

[Learn more](https://www.jetbrains.com/community/support/#section=communities)

### For users of competing tools 25% off

If you have a paid license for a product that competes with IntelliJ IDEA Ultimate, you qualify for a 25% discount on a standard commercial or personal subscription for IntelliJ IDEA Ultimate.

[Learn more](https://www.jetbrains.com/buy/competitive/?product=idea)

### 45-day trial for teams free

Teams within verified organizations can request a 45-day extended trial for an unlimited number of users. To submit a request, fill out the form at the link below.  
The trial license key will be provided once your request has been approved.

[Learn more](https://lp.jetbrains.com/intellij-idea-extended-trial/)

Do you create videos, social media or blog posts, articles, podcasts, email newsletters, or other digital content tailored for an audience interested in tech news, software reviews, developer tips, or tech industry topics? Join the JetBrains Content Creators Program to get a complimentary personal All Products Pack license and special offers for your audience.

[Learn more](https://www.jetbrains.com/community/content-creators/)
