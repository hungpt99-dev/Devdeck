# Code of Conduct (Contributor Covenant 2.1, adapted)

We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

Positive behavior: welcoming language, respect for differing views, constructive feedback, accepting responsibility, community-first decisions.

Unacceptable behavior: sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, harassment, publishing private information without permission.

Report privately to `conduct@hungpt99.dev`. Complaints are reviewed promptly and fairly. Consequences range from a private warning to a permanent ban. Adapted from https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html.
